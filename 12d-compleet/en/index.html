Process

How a project runs

  1. STEP-1

    Introduction

    A conversation in which you tell us what goes wrong or what you want to build. PlatforMaat tells you honestly whether it can help.

  2. STEP-2

    Quote

    With a fixed price and a fixed scope, no hourly rate. Every quote comes with a data processing agreement.

  3. STEP-3

    Building

    A maximum of two feedback rounds. Extra work is additional work and is agreed in writing before it starts.

  4. STEP-4

    Delivery and maintenance

    Delivery, training and a handover file. After that PlatforMaat can take care of hosting and maintenance.
